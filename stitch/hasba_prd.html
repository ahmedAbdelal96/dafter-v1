# Hasba: Premium Business & Accounting SaaS Mobile App PRD ## 1. Project Overview Hasba (حسبة) is a high-end SaaS platform designed for merchants and SMEs in the Egyptian and Arabic markets. It provides a comprehensive suite of tools for business management, accounting, and daily operations, delivered through a world-class mobile experience. ## 2. Target Audience * **Company Users:** Merchant owners, managers, accountants, and operational staff. * **Super Admins:** Platform administrators managing the entire SaaS ecosystem. ## 3. Design Philosophy & Style * **Tone:** Serious, trustworthy, efficient, premium, and polished. * **Visual Language:** Clean financial SaaS style, strong hierarchy, elegant spacing, and minimal clutter. * **Color Palette:** * Primary Accent: Premium Green (#1F7A5A) * Surfaces: White/Slate/Charcoal * Feedback: Blue (Info), Red (Risk/Overdue), Amber (Warning). * **UX Principles:** Mobile-first, thumb-friendly, fast navigation (1-2 taps for core actions), RTL-first (Arabic support). ## 4. Information Architecture (Company App) ### Bottom Navigation: 1. **Home:** Dashboard, summary KPIs, quick actions, recent activity. 2. **Customers:** List, search, details, balances, statement. 3. **Invoices:** List, filters (unpaid, paid, etc.), creation flow, details. 4. **Payments:** Receivables, record payment, distribution. 5. **More:** Suppliers, Employees, Products, Installments, Reports, Settings. ## 5. Core Features & Flows ### A. Company App Flows * **Authentication:** Splash, Login, PIN/Biometric. * **Dashboard:** Real-time business health (sales, collections, alerts). * **Customer Management:** Detailed profiles, payment behavior, and ledger. * **Invoice Creation:** Optimized, fast workflow with customer snapshot and product typeahead. * **Payment Tracking:** Receivable aging and payment distribution (FIFO). * **Ledger/Statements:** Chronological business history with export options. ### B. Super Admin App Flows * **Platform Dashboard:** Subscription metrics, platform health, and MRR. * **Company Management:** Directory of all SaaS clients, status control (suspend/activate). * **Subscription/Billing:** Managing plans and upgrade paths. * **Audit & Support:** Activity feeds and issue resolution. ## 6. Technical Requirements * Native-feel UX for both iOS and Android. * Full RTL (Arabic) and LTR (English) support. * Light and Dark mode support. * Optimized for modern safe areas. ## 7. Success Metrics * **Efficiency:** Time taken to create an invoice or record a payment. * **Clarity:** User ability to identify overdue receivables at a glance. * **Professionalism:** High visual polish reflecting brand trust.
