# Hasba Cash Reconciliation - Phase 2A Visibility Guide

## Scope
This document covers Phase 2A only.
Phase 1 behavior remains unchanged.

Phase 2A adds:
- History/list visibility for daily reconciliation records
- Readable daily detail view
- Operational exposure in dashboard
- Operational exposure in reports

Phase 2A does not add:
- Reopen workflow
- Mobile implementation
- Shift/session/POS workflows
- Ledger posting from reconciliation
- Merge with official totals

## Operational vs Official Boundary

### Official system-generated numbers
Generated by official system transactions:
- Invoices
- Payments
- Ledger
- Official reports and financial totals

### Operational reconciliation numbers
Operational-only inputs and calculations:
- Opening cash
- Cash sales outside system
- Cash expenses outside system
- Actual counted cash
- Expected cash
- Variance

Operational reconciliation numbers are displayed separately and must not be treated as official accounting totals.

## What Changed in Phase 2A

### 1) History/list view
- Endpoint: `GET /cash-reconciliation/daily/history`
- Supports filters:
  - `dateFrom`
  - `dateTo`
  - `status` (`DRAFT` / `CLOSED`)
  - pagination (`page`, `limit`)
- Web view shows:
  - business date
  - status
  - expected cash
  - actual cash
  - variance

### 2) Detail readability
- Endpoint: `GET /cash-reconciliation/daily/:id`
- Detail is grouped for readability:
  - Inputs
  - Calculated values
  - Audit information
- Closed records remain immutable.

### 3) Dashboard operational card
- A separate operational-only card shows reconciliation summary.
- Card includes counts and total variance.
- Card explicitly states operational-only boundary.

### 4) Reports operational visibility
- Reports page includes a separate operational section for cash reconciliation.
- Displays summary metrics plus latest closed records list.
- Section clearly states separation from official report totals.

## Permissions and Safety
- Read visibility uses protected read access.
- Create/update/close remains owner-only as in Phase 1.
- No changes to entitlement/security model.

## Operator Guidance
1. Keep company mode as required (`DISABLED` or `SIMPLE_DAILY`).
2. Use daily screen to create/update/close records.
3. Use history view to review past dates and statuses.
4. Use dashboard/reports operational sections for visibility only.
5. Do not use reconciliation values as replacement for official accounting numbers.

## QA Focus for Phase 2A
- History filters and pagination work correctly.
- Detail endpoint and detail UI render complete grouped data.
- Draft/closed distinction is clear and consistent.
- Dashboard and reports show operational data with explicit labels.
- No ledger posting.
- No official totals merge.
- Phase 1 create/update/close behavior remains intact.

## Known Caveats
- Existing Arabic wording/encoding caveat remains non-blocking.
- `CASH-P2A` intentionally keeps `WEB-03` (from Phase 1) deferred unchanged.
